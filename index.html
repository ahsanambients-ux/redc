REDC strategic framework · Six presentations

A shared strategic reference for understanding REDC, defining its direction and aligning how the institution presents and delivers its value.

The six presentations move from institutional foundations to brand understanding, communication, governance, strategy and future direction. Together, they provide leadership and teams with a common view of what REDC stands for, whom it serves, how it should communicate and what must guide its next stage.

Why this matters: A clearer vision, mission and positioning give teams a consistent basis for decisions. Shared messages reduce fragmentation across programmes and channels. Defined principles and governance clarify responsibilities, while the future-direction work creates a basis for coordinated priorities, stronger collaboration and measurable execution.
